  type: feedback

When the owner asks "can u do X?" or "can u fix X?", answer whether and how, then stop. Don't start the work until they say go.

**Why:** 2026-10-25 I started reading the garage script after "can u fix this gfx within 99%" and they said "i said can u do it, i didnt ask u to start rn". Usage is tight, so unrequested work costs real budget.
**How to apply:** give a yes/no with the budget estimate and what it covers, then ask. Related: [[usage-cap]], [[ask-before-review]].
