For extra characters in a scene (e.g. idol group on stage), use normal real Roblox avatars that fit the vibe (e.g. male streetwear), fetched via avatar-3d and rigged like the main avatar. Don't try to replicate real people (photo face heads, member-named merch) and don't hand-build mannequin bodies with primitive details.

**Why:** 2026-10-27 Suhaa concert: owner called mannequin members "weird", photo-real Cortis member heads "why would u add their heads", and the per-member matching "utter nonsense"; "just make it normal… street wear… men only".
**How to apply:** search Roblox users by vibe keywords (users/search), contact-sheet thumbnails (tools/user_sheet.py), pick ones matching the requested look/gender, cache with tools/cache_avatar.py. Not from the crowd/audience pool. Related: [[session-is-own-project]], [[no-rbx-motion-clips]].
